Where is this wall?

The place sets the clock, sunrise and sunset, and the weather. Everything after this has sensible defaults, and all of it can be changed later in config/config.toml.

    Work hours

    During work hours the wall shows a calm work face: the time, your next meeting, a weather line. The rest of the time it drifts into a slow ambient scene.

    Look

    Pick a scene. The previews are live, with made-up data. Number keys on the wall switch themes any time.

    For a dim projector: lifts the darkest tones, true black stays black. G on the wall cycles it.

    Your data (all optional)

    Weather is always on. Anything you leave out simply isn't drawn.

    News headlines

    One headline at a time on the work face; urgent ones (warnings, evacuations) stand out.

    Calendar

    Your next meeting with a countdown, and "leave early" when rain is due before it.

    Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. Use the secret address, not the public one. It is saved in config/secrets.toml, never in config.toml, and only its host ever shows up in logs.

    Computers to watch

    A quiet mark per machine; one that stops answering pings for two minutes stands out.

    Spotify now playing

    The track that is playing, with its progress and album art.

    Connect Spotify

    One time, on a PC with a browser (about five minutes): create an app at developer.spotify.com/dashboard with the redirect URI http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback, then from the repo run python scripts/spotify_auth.py --client-id <your client id>. It prints three values: paste them here. Step by step: docs/SOURCES.md, "Spotify". They are saved in config/secrets.toml.

    More, later

    Finances (a Google Sheet), GitHub project pulse, Last.fm, pushes from phones and scripts, and your own sources are set up in config/config.toml: the file this wizard writes has commented examples, and docs/SOURCES.md explains each one.
